**SOLMARA** — an original sun-soaked open-world crime game. Drive and steal cars, dodge the law, run missions across the fictional coastal city of Solmara. Explore on foot and by car, weave through traffic and pedestrians, build your wanted level and outrun the police, take on a story of getaway jobs and rival crews. All art is drawn in code and all sound is synthesized — no external assets, no network needed.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD to move/drive, E to enter or exit a car, mouse to aim, click to shoot, Shift to run, P to pause, M to mute. Progress saves locally.
